Privacy

PR Forge · last updated 3 November 2026

PR Forge is a free workout log made by one person, for their own training and their friends’. It isn't a business, it has no ads, and your data is never sold or shared.

What PR Forge keeps

  • Your Google account’s email address and name, so you can sign in and only you can see your data. PR Forge never sees your Google password.
  • Your training data: workouts, sets, weights and reps, routines, custom exercises, and settings such as lb/kg and rest timer.

Nothing else is collected: no location, contacts, analytics or tracking.

Where it’s stored

  • On your phone, in the browser’s storage, so the app works without signal.
  • In a database run by Supabase, so your workouts are backed up and come back on a new phone. The database only lets a signed-in person read and write their own records.

Sign-in is handled by Google and Supabase. Their own privacy policies apply to what they do: Google, Supabase.

Who can see it

Only you, when signed in. When you sign in from the iPhone home-screen app, a temporary sign-in key is kept in the database for up to 5 minutes so the app can pick it up, then deleted. The person who runs PR Forge has administrator access to the database and could technically look at the records, but won’t except to fix a problem you’ve asked about.

Deleting your data

  • In the app, go to Progress → Settings & data → Erase all my data. That deletes your workouts and settings from your phone and from the backup.
  • To have your sign-in record (email and name) removed as well, open a request at github.com/kevo00012/pr-forge/issues or ask the person who sent you PR Forge.
